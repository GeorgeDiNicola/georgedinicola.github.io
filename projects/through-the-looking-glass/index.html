A note on the chess

Carroll’s game departs from ordinary chess in several ways: turns do not always alternate, a check goes unanswered, and Queens “castle.” These may be mistakes or deliberate touches that suit the odd logic of the Looking-Glass world. (Carroll’s preface)

First published in 1871, Through the Looking-Glass has been in the public domain in the United States for more than 50 years. (Project Gutenberg)
